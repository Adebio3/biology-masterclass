Section A: Multiple choice

Choose one answer for each question. Then press Mark my answers.

  1. A diploid cell…

    Answer: C. Human body cells: 2n = 46.

  2. Homologous chromosomes…

    Answer: C. They may carry different alleles.

  3. Meiosis is necessary in gamete production because…

    Answer: A. Reduction division.

  4. In which stage of meiosis do homologous chromosomes separate?

    Answer: C. Sister chromatids separate in anaphase II.

  5. Crossing over occurs during…

    Answer: B. At chiasmata.

  6. Independent assortment results from…

    Answer: D. 2ⁿ combinations.

  7. An allele is…

    Answer: A. Locus is the position.

  8. Tt × tt gives…

    Answer: D. This is a test cross.

  9. Codominance means…

    Answer: C. Example: HbᴬHbˢ.

  10. Parents IᴬIᵒ × IᴮIᵒ can have children with blood groups…

    Answer: C. Each in a ratio of 1 : 1 : 1 : 1.

  11. A carrier woman (XᴴXʰ) and a normal man (XᴴY). Probability of a son with haemophilia?

    Answer: B. Sons get Xʰ from mother half the time.

  12. AaBb × AaBb with unlinked genes gives a phenotypic ratio of…

    Answer: B. Independent assortment.

  13. In a test cross of AaBb, many more parental phenotypes than recombinants suggests…

    Answer: A. Recombinants arise only by crossing over.

  14. Epistasis is…

    Answer: D. Changes expected ratios.

  15. In a χ² test with 4 classes, the degrees of freedom are…

    Answer: A. Classes − 1.

  16. Albinism is caused by a recessive allele of…

    Answer: D. Non-functional tyrosinase.

  17. Huntington’s disease is caused by…

    Answer: A. Huntingtin protein is abnormal.

  18. In peas, le le plants are short because…

    Answer: C. Little active GA is made.

  19. In the lac operon, when lactose is absent…

    Answer: B. The enzymes are inducible.

  20. Gibberellin causes genes to be transcribed by…

    Answer: B. Releases transcription factors.

Section B: Paper 4 style

Write your answers in your exercise book, then check against the mark scheme.

Question 1 [9 marks]

  1. Describe the behaviour of chromosomes during meiosis. [6]
  2. Explain how meiosis produces genetic variation. [3]
Show mark scheme
  • (a) prophase I: homologous pairs / bivalents; crossing over; metaphase I: bivalents on equator; anaphase I: homologues separate; telophase I: two haploid cells; metaphase II: chromosomes on equator; anaphase II: centromeres divide, chromatids separate; four haploid cells [max 6]
  • (b) crossing over: new allele combinations; independent assortment of homologues at metaphase I; random orientation of chromatids at metaphase II [max 3]

Question 2 [6 marks]

Haemophilia is caused by a recessive allele of the F8 gene on the X chromosome.
  1. Draw a genetic diagram for a carrier woman and an unaffected man. [4]
  2. Explain why haemophilia is more common in males. [2]
Show mark scheme
  • (a) parents XᴴXʰ × XᴴY; gametes Xᴴ, Xʰ and Xᴴ, Y; offspring XᴴXᴴ, XᴴY, XᴴXʰ, XʰY; phenotypes: normal female, normal male, carrier female, haemophiliac male [4]
  • (b) males have one X chromosome; one recessive allele expressed / no second allele to mask it [2]

Question 3 [6 marks]

In a dihybrid test cross the results were 182, 176, 21 and 21.
  1. Calculate χ² for an expected 1 : 1 : 1 : 1 ratio. [3]
  2. Explain what the result shows. [3]
Show mark scheme
  • (a) E = 100 each; (O−E)²/E: 67.24, 57.76, 62.41, 62.41; χ² = 249.8 [3]
  • (b) df = 3; critical value 7.82; χ² much larger; significant difference; reject null hypothesis; genes are linked; few recombinants by crossing over [max 3]

Question 4 [7 marks]

  1. Distinguish between structural genes and regulatory genes. [2]
  2. Explain how the lac operon is switched on when lactose is present. [5]
Show mark scheme
  • (a) structural: codes for protein with a function in the cell, e.g. enzyme; regulatory: codes for protein that controls expression of other genes, e.g. repressor [2]
  • (b) lactose binds to repressor; changes shape; cannot bind operator; RNA polymerase binds promoter; lacZ, lacY, lacA transcribed; β-galactosidase and permease made [max 5]

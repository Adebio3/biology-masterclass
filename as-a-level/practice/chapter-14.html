Section A: Multiple choice

Choose one answer for each question. Then press Mark my answers.

  1. What is homeostasis?

    Answer: D. Examples: core temperature, blood glucose and blood water potential.

  2. Negative feedback…

    Answer: C. Receptor → coordination → effector → corrective response.

  3. Urea is formed from…

    Answer: C. Deamination of excess amino acids gives ammonia.

  4. Which region of the kidney contains the loops of Henle?

    Answer: D. Glomeruli and convoluted tubules are in the cortex.

  5. Which is filtered into the Bowman’s capsule?

    Answer: A. Large proteins and cells stay in the blood.

  6. Which acts as the filter in ultrafiltration?

    Answer: D. The basement membrane holds back large proteins.

  7. Why is the hydrostatic pressure high in the glomerulus?

    Answer: C. Blood enters faster than it leaves.

  8. How is glucose reabsorbed in the PCT?

    Answer: D. Secondary active transport.

  9. Why do PCT cells have microvilli?

    Answer: B. Many mitochondria also supply ATP.

  10. Osmoreceptors are found in the…

    Answer: B. They detect blood water potential.

  11. ADH acts on the…

    Answer: C. It increases permeability to water.

  12. ADH increases permeability by…

    Answer: D. More water channels in the membrane.

  13. After drinking a lot of water…

    Answer: A. Blood water potential rises.

  14. Insulin is secreted by…

    Answer: B. In response to high blood glucose.

  15. Glucagon stimulates…

    Answer: A. Blood glucose rises.

  16. In the glucagon pathway, the second messenger is…

    Answer: C. Made by adenylyl cyclase.

  17. The enzyme cascade…

    Answer: B. Each enzyme activates many of the next.

  18. Glucose test strips contain…

    Answer: C. A colour change shows glucose concentration.

  19. Stomata open when…

    Answer: B. Proton pumps drive K⁺ uptake.

  20. ABA causes stomata to close by…

    Answer: B. Ca²⁺ acts as a second messenger.

Section B: Paper 4 style

Write your answers in your exercise book, then check against the mark scheme.

Question 1 [6 marks]

  1. Explain the principle of negative feedback. [3]
  2. Describe how urea is formed. [3]
Show mark scheme
  • (a) change from set point detected by receptor; information to effector via nervous / endocrine system; effector reverses change; returns to set point [max 3]
  • (b) excess amino acids; deamination in liver; amine group removed as ammonia; ammonia + CO₂ → urea (ornithine cycle) [max 3]

Question 2 [9 marks]

  1. Describe how ultrafiltration occurs in the Bowman’s capsule. [5]
  2. Explain how glucose is reabsorbed in the proximal convoluted tubule. [4]
Show mark scheme
  • (a) high hydrostatic pressure; efferent arteriole narrower; through pores in endothelium; basement membrane as filter; between podocytes; small molecules pass, proteins and cells retained [max 5]
  • (b) Na⁺/K⁺ pump on blood side; Na⁺ concentration in cell low; Na⁺ and glucose enter by co-transport; glucose diffuses into blood; facilitated diffusion; microvilli / mitochondria [max 4]

Question 3 [7 marks]

  1. Describe the role of ADH when blood water potential falls. [5]
  2. Explain why the effect of ADH is an example of negative feedback. [2]
Show mark scheme
  • (a) osmoreceptors in hypothalamus; posterior pituitary releases more ADH; ADH binds receptors on collecting duct cells; aquaporins inserted; more water reabsorbed by osmosis; small volume of concentrated urine [max 5]
  • (b) blood water potential returns towards set point; less ADH then released [2]

Question 4 [9 marks]

  1. Outline how glucagon stimulates the release of glucose from liver cells. [5]
  2. Explain how stomata close when a plant is short of water. [4]
Show mark scheme
  • (a) glucagon binds receptor; G-protein activated; adenylyl cyclase; cAMP second messenger; protein kinase A; enzyme cascade / amplification; glycogen → glucose [max 5]
  • (b) ABA released; binds receptors on guard cells; Ca²⁺ enters cytoplasm as second messenger; K⁺ leaves; water potential rises; water leaves by osmosis; guard cells flaccid [max 4]
